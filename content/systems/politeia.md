## What it is

Ardent Politeia starts from one institutional friction and connects the approved systems, workflows, and optional agent harnesses needed to address it. The source describes a typed operating model, authorized operations, evidence-bound consequential transitions, and projections that cannot grant themselves new authority.

## Current source

The public repository is a starter architecture, not a feature-complete product. Its [first vertical slice](https://github.com/ardent-tools/politeia/blob/main/docs/18-FIRST_VERTICAL_SLICE.md) specifies a fresh client-controlled workspace, bounded reconnaissance, owner approval, a derived operational generation, an authorized effect, independent verification, handoff, and commissioner revocation. The slice is the next proof target, not a completed demonstration.

## What's open

The first vertical slice has not established its complete acceptance path. The site has no cast, release, or deployment receipt for Ardent Politeia. The source's [engineering handoff](https://github.com/ardent-tools/politeia/blob/main/docs/20-ENGINEERING_HANDOFF.md) names artifact-bound `POL-A` through `POL-L` receipts as the starter-phase exit criteria.
